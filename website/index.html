OldVis Image Taxonomy Labeler

A web-based system for taxonomy labeling and comparison of old visualization images.

Taxonomy labeling

Assign hierarchical labels, such as vis → bar chart → stacked bar chart, to a visualization image.

Taxonomy comparison

Compare taxonomy labels exported from the labeling interface across multiple annotators.
